How it works

Three steps on each side of the desk

OfficeLog it, hand it back

  1. Snap 2–3 photosAI reads the photos and fills in the name, color, brand and search words. Staff fix anything off.
  2. Say where it's stored"Main office, bin B." Students never see this. The app gives a tag like LF-6514 for a sticky note.
  3. Approve the claimCompare the student's proof with the photos. Approve, hand it over, done. Other claims on it close automatically.

StudentFind it, claim it

  1. Sign in with the school accountThe same Google account as Classroom and Chromebooks. No new password.
  2. Describe what you lost"Blue water bottle with a soccer sticker." Matches show with photos. Not there? Tap Search with AI.
  3. Tap "This is mine"Add a detail only the owner would know. When the office approves, pick it up with the tag number.

Built to keep AI costs low

AI only when it earns its keep

Most searches never touch AI. Five design choices keep the bill close to zero and impossible to run away.

$0

Word search first

Every search starts free. It knows synonyms, so "sweatshirt" finds hoodies and "specs" finds glasses.

On tap

AI only when asked

Students tap Search with AI only when the word search misses. That covers the hard cases.

Free repeat

Remembers answers

The same AI search on an unchanged shelf reuses the earlier answer at no cost.

1 call

One description per item

AI reads the photos once, after the last one is added. Adding or removing photos doesn't call it again.

Capped

Limits you set

20 AI searches per student a day, plus a monthly spend limit on the AI account as a hard ceiling.

Safe by design

Built for student privacy

  • School accounts onlyPersonal Gmail can't sign in. Basic profile only, never Drive or Gmail.
  • Storage spots stay privateOnly staff see where items are kept. Students can't see each other's claims.
  • No location data in photosGPS is stripped from every phone photo on upload.
  • No student names to AIAI sees item photos and search text. Names and emails stay in the school's database.
