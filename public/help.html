For students

Find your item

  1. Sign in with your school Google accountThe same one you use for Classroom and Chromebooks.
  2. Describe what you lostType it the way you'd say it, like "blue water bottle with a soccer sticker". Matches show up with photos.
  3. Not there? Tap "Search with AI"AI reads every item on the shelf and catches things described in different words. You get 20 AI searches a day.

Claim it and pick it up

  1. Tap "This is mine"Add your name, grade, and something only the owner would know: what's inside, a scratch, writing on it.
  2. Wait for the officeYour claim shows under My claims. The office compares your details with the photos.
  3. Pick it upWhen it says Approved, go to the office and ask for the tag number, like LF-6514.

Search tips

  • Say what it is first: "hoodie", "calculator", "lunch box".
  • Add what makes it yours: brand, color, size, stickers, your initials.
  • Keep it short. "Black Nike hoodie size M" works better than a long story.
  • Nothing yet? Items get added every day. Check back tomorrow.

For office staff

Log a found item (about a minute)

  1. Take 2–3 photosOn a phone, tap the photo box to open the camera. Show the whole item, then close-ups of brand, stickers, writing or damage.
  2. Type where you're storing itFor example "Main office, bin B". Students never see this. Earlier spots are suggested as you type.
  3. Check the descriptionAI fills in the name, category, color, details and search words from the photos. Fix anything that's off.
  4. Add search wordsIn Other words a student might use, add a few, like "sweatshirt, jacket". This helps students find it.
  5. Save, then tag the itemWrite the tag number it shows, like LF-6514, on a sticky note and put it with the item.

Review claims

  1. Open the Claims tabThe yellow number shows how many claims are waiting.
  2. Compare the proof with the photosThe student's detail, the item on file and its storage spot are side by side.
  3. Approve or declineApprove & mark returned tells the student to pick it up and closes any other claims on that item. Decline tells the student it wasn't approved.

Keep the shelf tidy

  • On the shelf: everything waiting for an owner. Tap Change to update a storage spot.
  • 30+ days: items to donate or discard. Delete them once they leave the shelf.
  • Mark returned when you hand back an item without a claim in the app.
  • Preview student view shows exactly what students see.

Questions

Why can't I sign in with my personal Gmail?
Only school accounts can sign in, so only people at your school can see items and make claims.
Why can't students see where an item is stored?
So items are only handed back by the office, after a claim is checked.
What does the AI see?
Item photos, item descriptions and the words typed into search. It never sees student names or emails. Location data is removed from every photo.
Why is there a daily limit on AI searches?
Each AI search has a small cost. Word search is free and unlimited, and finds most items.
Two students claimed the same item. What happens?
Staff approve the claim with the best proof. The other claims close automatically with a note that the item was returned to its owner.
